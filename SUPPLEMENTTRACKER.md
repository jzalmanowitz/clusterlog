Add a suppliment tracker

Purpose of this is to track the user's adherance to the vitamin d3 regimen.  The vitamin D3 vitamin regimen was developed by a fellow clusterhead and has helped many people in this community reduce the frequency, severity, and duration of their attacks. At a high level, it combines high-dose vitamin D3 with a set of cofactors and anti-inflammatory supplements taken daily. As with any supplement regimen, discuss it with your healthcare provider before beginning.

##Background Information##

Supplement | Dose
Vitamin D3 | 10,000 IU/day
Omega 3 Fish Oil | 1000 to 2400 mg/day (Minimum of EPA 360 mg/day, DHA 240 mg/day)
Calcium	Citrate | 220 to 500 mg/day
Magnesium | 400 to 500 mg/day (magnesium malate, magnesium glycinate or magnesium citrate)
Vitamin K2 (MK-4 & MK-7) | MK-4 1000 mcg/day, MK-7 200 mcg/day (MK-7 preferred due to half-life)
Vitamin A | 900 mcg (3,000 IU) for men, 700 mcg (2,333 IU) for women
Vitamin B-50 | (Take for three months only, the Mature Multi-vitamin will be sufficient after that)
Zinc | 10 mg/day
Boron | 1 mg/day


##Two-Week Loading Schedule##
Week 1: 50,000 IU/day vitamin D3 for seven days. Take all other supplements and cofactors daily.
Week 2: 40,000 IU/day vitamin D3 for six days, then drop to 10,000 IU/day on the seventh day. This becomes your ongoing maintenance dose. Take all other supplements and cofactors daily.

##Four-Week Loading Schedule##
Week 1: 20,000 IU/day vitamin D3 plus one (1) loading dose of 50,000 IU/week vitamin D3
Week 2: 20,000 IU/day vitamin D3 plus one (1) loading dose of 50,000 IU/week vitamin D3
Week 3: 15,000 IU/day vitamin D3 and no loading dose
Week 4: 15,000 IU/day vitamin D3 and no loading dose

At the end of week four, drop to 10,000 IU/day as your ongoing maintenance dose. Take all supplements and cofactors daily throughout.
